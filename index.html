Payment Help

Why is my name locked?

Payment account name must match the verified account/KYC name.

How will QR verification work?

In production, supported QR data will be read and checked against the verified account name before adding the payment method.

Why can't I see a payment method?

Admin can turn countries and individual Bank/Wallet/QR/Worldwide providers on or off. Disabled methods are hidden.

Why does Coming Soon appear?

If no available payment method is enabled for a visible country/category, Coming Soon appears automatically.
